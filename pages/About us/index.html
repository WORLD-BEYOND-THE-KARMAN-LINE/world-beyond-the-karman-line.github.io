A non-profit magazine sharing the science and stories of space.

Our Mission

World Beyond the Karman Line is a non-profit space magazine led by a team of space enthusiasts, educators, and science writers. We are dedicated to inspiring curiosity and expanding horizons beyond Earth's edge.

We exist to share knowledge, spark imagination, and celebrate space science and exploration. We do not generate revenue or operate commercially; our mission is driven by passion, learning, and the spirit of discovery.

Join us as we explore the wonders of the universe, one issue at a time.
